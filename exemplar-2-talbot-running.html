Why I Like Running

Running is one of my favourite hobbies because it helps me stay fit, it's a great way to spend time outdoors, and you can steadily work towards new goals.

I enjoy challenging myself and seeing improvement over time. I look forward to Sports Day every year!

Health Benefits of Running

Running can improve fitness, strengthen the heart and help reduce stress. It's a simple activity that can be done almost anywhere and requires very little equipment. Although you do want a good pair of shoes.

In the photo above I am running at Nantu Wama Parkrun in North Adelaide. 'Nantu Wama' is a word from the Kaurna language which means 'horse plain'. On some days you can see the horses in the paddock that the Parkrun course goes around.

Tips for Beginner Runners

Here are some of my tips for beginner runners. I have made lots of mistakes over my many years of running so heed my advice:

  • Start slowly.
  • Wear comfortable and supportive shoes.
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Take some rest days.
  • Focus on being consistent.
  • Find some friends to run with.

If you follow this advice you'll be running like me in no time!

The Adelaide marathon

This year (2026) I ran my first marathon - the Adelaide Marathon. I trained for a long time leading up to it and on the day it was very hard, but I felt very proud (and tired) after completing it.

My final time was 4:05:20!

The day after this photo was taken I was extremely sore, but I already want to run it again next year.
